# Target SQL Business Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Target e-commerce data using SQL to understand customer distribution, order trends, payment behavior, freight costs, delivery performance, and regional business patterns.

The analysis was performed using SQL queries on multiple relational tables including customers, orders, payments, and order items.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this case study is to answer key business questions related to:

- Customer distribution across cities and states
- Order growth trends over time
- Monthly and seasonal order patterns
- Payment behavior and installment usage
- Freight cost analysis
- Delivery performance across regions

## Key Insights

- Orders increased significantly from 2016 to 2018.
- Customer concentration was highest in São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais.
- Afternoon was the peak time for order placement.
- Total order payment value increased by 136.98% from Jan-Aug 2017 to Jan-Aug 2018.
- São Paulo had the highest total order value and freight value.
- Remote states had higher average freight costs and longer delivery times.
- Credit card was the most preferred payment method.
- Most customers preferred paying in one installment.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing and logistics investment in high-performing states like São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais.
- Improve logistics infrastructure in remote states with high freight costs and long delivery times.
- Prioritize afternoon and night campaigns since most orders are placed during these periods.
- Optimize delivery estimates to improve customer trust and satisfaction.
- Continue supporting credit card payments and one-time payment options due to high customer preference.
